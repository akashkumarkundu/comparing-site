# Support & FAQ | Compare Anything

Welcome to **Compare Anything** support. We are here to help you make informed decisions across the web without switching tabs.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How does Compare Anything work?
1. Open any product, course, job offer, or service page in Chrome.
2. Click the **Compare Anything** extension icon and click **`[ + ADD TO COMPARISON ]`**.
3. Repeat for 1 to 3 additional webpages (supports 2 to 4 pages total).
4. Optionally enter what matters to you (e.g., *"Best for programming under Tk 80,000"*).
5. Click **`[ COMPARE {n} PAGES ]`** to view a full-page, side-by-side comparison table.

### 2. Is any registration or credit card required?
No. Compare Anything requires no registration, no account creation, and no credit card. It is completely free to install and use.

### 3. What websites work with Compare Anything?
The extension works on virtually any public webpage containing structured or text information, including:
- E-commerce stores (Star Tech, Ryans, Amazon, Daraz)
- SaaS pricing pages (Linear, Jira, Slack, Notion)
- Job portals (RemoteOK, LinkedIn, WeWorkRemotely)
- Educational platforms (Coursera, edX, HarvardX)
- Cloud & hosting providers (DigitalOcean, AWS, Linode)

*Note: Internal Chrome browser pages (like `chrome://settings` or `chrome://extensions`) cannot be read for security reasons.*

### 4. What does "Not stated" in the comparison table mean?
Compare Anything adheres strictly to our **Anti-Hallucination Policy**. If a website does not specify an attribute (such as weight or warranty), the AI engine writes **"Not stated"** instead of guessing or making up numbers.

### 5. What are the daily limits?
To prevent automated abuse and maintain fast performance, users can run up to **10 free comparisons per day**.
